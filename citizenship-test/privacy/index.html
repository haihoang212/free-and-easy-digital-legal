Privacy Policy

Canadian Citizenship Test Prep
Developed by Free & Easy Digital

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Important: Canadian Citizenship Test Prep is an independent educational study application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of Canada.

1. Overview

Free & Easy Digital respects your privacy. Canadian Citizenship Test Prep is designed to help users study for the Canadian citizenship test while minimizing the collection of personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, or mailing address to use its study features.

2. Study Progress and App Settings

Certain information such as study progress, practice results, preferences, saved sessions, and app settings may be stored locally on your device so that the app can remember your choices and provide its study features.

Free & Easy Digital does not receive this locally stored study-progress information through an app account or Free & Easy Digital server.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Canadian Citizenship Test Prep uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. During testing, the app may use Google-provided test or demo ad units. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still automatically collect or share certain data when it is present in the app.

Data that may be collected or shared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK includes:

  • Approximate location, such as general location inferred from an IP address.
  • App interactions, such as ad-related or app interaction events.
  • Diagnostics, such as information used to monitor performance, reliability, and errors.
  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, or similar identifiers made available by the device or platform.

This data may be used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Free & Easy Digital does not use the app to request your precise GPS location for advertising.

Google processes data under its own privacy policies and terms. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices and advertising controls through Google's privacy resources and Android settings.

4. Personal Information

The app does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, postal address, government identification number, contacts, personal photos, or text messages as part of its normal study features.

Free & Easy Digital does not sell personal information collected through the app.

5. Audio and Text-to-Speech Features

Canadian Citizenship Test Prep includes audio study features that may use Android's text-to-speech services.

Text-to-speech functionality may be provided by the text-to-speech engine installed or selected on your Android device. Any processing performed by that service is subject to the privacy practices of the service provider. Free & Easy Digital does not receive recordings of your voice through the app's text-to-speech study feature.

6. Permissions

The app is designed to request only permissions that are necessary for its features. It does not require access to your contacts, personal photographs, text messages, or precise location for its core study functions.

7. Children

Canadian Citizenship Test Prep is intended as a general educational study tool for people preparing for the Canadian citizenship test. It is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through an app account or Free & Easy Digital study-profile system.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Canadian Citizenship Test Prep does not create a Free & Easy Digital user account and does not maintain server-side study-progress records for users.

Locally stored app data can normally be removed by clearing the app's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Data processed by Google Mobile Ads is handled according to Google's own data retention, privacy, and account or device-control practices. Free & Easy Digital does not currently provide a separate in-app request process for deleting data held by Google through the Mobile Ads SDK.

9. External Information and Government Sources

The app may contain educational information based on publicly available Canadian citizenship study material and may provide references or links to external resources.

External websites and services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Free & Easy Digital is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. When the policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.
