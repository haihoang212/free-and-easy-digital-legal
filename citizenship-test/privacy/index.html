Important: Canadian Citizenship Test Prep is an independent educational study application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of Canada.

1. Overview

Free & Easy Digital respects your privacy. Canadian Citizenship Test Prep is designed to help users study for the Canadian citizenship test while minimizing the collection of personal information.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of Canadian Citizenship Test Prep does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, or mailing address.

Free & Easy Digital does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through the app.

3. Study Progress and App Settings

Certain information such as study progress, preferences, or app settings may be stored locally on your device so that the app can remember your choices and provide its study features.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to Free & Easy Digital.

4. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of the app does not contain third-party advertising and does not use advertising trackers.

The current version also does not use third-party analytics services to build advertising profiles or track users across other apps or websites.

5. Audio and Text-to-Speech Features

Canadian Citizenship Test Prep includes audio study features that may use Android's text-to-speech services.

Text-to-speech functionality may be provided by the text-to-speech engine installed or selected on your Android device. Any processing performed by that service is subject to the privacy practices of the service provider. Free & Easy Digital does not receive recordings of your voice through the app's text-to-speech study feature.

6. Permissions

The app is designed to request only permissions that are necessary for its features. It does not require access to your contacts, personal photographs, text messages, or precise location for its core study functions.

7. Children

Canadian Citizenship Test Prep is intended as a general educational study tool for people preparing for the Canadian citizenship test. It is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current version of the app.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current version does not create a Free & Easy Digital user account or transmit personal study data to us, Free & Easy Digital does not maintain a server-side account record for users of the app.

Locally stored app data can normally be removed by clearing the app's storage through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

9. External Information and Government Sources

The app may contain educational information based on publicly available Canadian citizenship study material and may provide references or links to external resources.

External websites and services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Free & Easy Digital is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. When the policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will also be updated.
